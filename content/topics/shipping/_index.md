Between what sits on my machine and what arrives on the web are stages nobody ever looks at.
These posts are about what happens after the writing — from the commit to the page that gets served.

The subject is not this blog but the pipeline behind it: a step that only runs in CI is permanently untested, and that holds for a minifier just as much as for a bundler, a container image or a compression step.

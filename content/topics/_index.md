> **Full disclosure:** every post on this blog is written using [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code), and most of them are about working with it.
> The commands, measurements and numbers you find here come from real sessions on my own machines. I read, check and edit everything before it goes up.
> Where something turned out to be wrong, the post says so.
> The writing style of the German pages is my own. The English versions are translated from them and follow that voice only as far as English allows: what they say is identical, how they sound is not.

**Two recurring themes run through everything here.**

The first: **working with AI is standard equipment, not an experiment — as ordinary as version control and automated tests.**
The scale and pace of this work can no longer be sustained without these tools.
So the interesting question isn't whether to use them, but how: which tasks you hand over, and how the results stay verifiable.

The second: **measure, don't assert.**
A number from a real run beats any plausible guess, including my own.
Where something is claimed here, it says what it was measured against, and where a measurement proved me wrong, the post says so.

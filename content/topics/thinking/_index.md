I cut tasks differently, I think in decisions rather than in steps, and I write down what used to be habit.
What working with an agent does to the way you think, and what it costs when fewer and fewer people have made the same experience.

This is the one topic here where I do not measure: the subject is my own head, and I have no checker for that.
Where others have measured, I point at their numbers.

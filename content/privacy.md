## Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.
When you open a page, your browser sends technically necessary data to GitHub, including your IP address, the time of the request, the address requested, and details about your browser and operating system.

Processing also takes place in the USA.

## Audience measurement

I want to know which posts get read, and use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com) for that.
The service is run by Martin Tournoij in Ireland; the servers are at Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany.
The data therefore never leaves the European Union.

GoatCounter sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser — not in local storage, not by any other means.
It records the page you opened, the referring source, browser, operating system, screen width, language, and the country derived from your IP address.

Your IP address is not stored permanently.
It is held in memory together with your browser identifier for at most eight hours, so that repeated requests within one session are not counted twice, and is converted into a random string in the process.
No personal reference can be reconstructed from it.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a data-minimising analysis of traffic (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
See [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for more.

## Comments

Below the posts — and only there — [giscus](https://giscus.app) is embedded.
Loading that section makes your browser connect to `giscus.app`, which necessarily transmits your IP address.
On every other page, giscus is not loaded at all.

The comments themselves live in the GitHub Discussions of this blog's repository and are publicly visible.
To write one you need a GitHub account and must authorise the giscus application.
giscus then places an encrypted access token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in; the token leaves your browser only to be decrypted for requests to the GitHub API.
As long as you do not write anything, no token is set.
By its own account, giscus collects no data and sets no cookies.

GitHub is the controller for the processing of your comment and your profile name.
You can edit or delete a comment at any time directly in GitHub Discussions.

The legal basis for loading the comment section is my legitimate interest in offering a way to respond to the posts (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## What does not happen here

No contact form and no newsletter.
No fonts from Google or anyone else: the site uses system fonts and files from its own server.
